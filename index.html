🎯 Rules of the road

  • 🇮🇳 Traffic keeps LEFT. Bus stops are on the left kerb.
  • 🚏 Stop fully inside the yellow BUS STOP bay, then press SPACE. Passengers pay fare when boarding.
  • 🏁 Follow the blue GPS route to drop passengers at their stop — smooth driving earns tips.
  • 🚦 Jumping a red light = ₹1000 fine. Speed limit 50 km/h — speed cameras everywhere.
  • ⛽ Diesel costs ₹95/L. Park beside an orange pump and hold F.
  • 🌧️ Rain: wet roads, longer braking and less grip. Crashes cost repair money.
